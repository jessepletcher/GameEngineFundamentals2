Player-facing policy

- Equipment changes and purchased upgrades affect future shots. Each launched
  volley captures its ball, golfer, course revision, launch settings and reward/XP
  multipliers. Later equipment changes or level-ups cannot reprice that shot.
- Firework fragments inherit the parent's context, including payout compensation
  when distant fireworks use fewer scoring fragments. Pinball chains retain the
  original ball's behavior and rewards throughout the chain.
- Course switching immediately cancels every outgoing ball, scoring/cosmetic
  fragment and pinball chain. Cancelled objects lose collision and group membership
  before deferred deletion; returning to the original course cannot revive them.
  Already earned money and XP remain. Cancelled shots receive no new payout.
- Partially damaged destructibles retain each hit's original money/XP contribution
  through course visits. Destruction pays their sum. The finishing shot's golfer
  determines respawn delay. Respawns reset these contributions.
- Market deposits retain their originating XP multiplier through growth and later
  cashout, including course-switch cashout. Cashout clears both money and XP once.
- In-game help explains equipment and course switching.

Implementation

GameState.capture_shot_context creates read-only snapshots; add_xp can accept an
explicit captured multiplier. Main launches and Ball fragments share the snapshot.
Landing, golden, pinball and destructible rewards use that context. Landing has a
duplicate-payment guard. Course revision and cancelled-state checks reject stale
callbacks. Pinball uses ball-bound tweens with completion callbacks so cancellation
does not strand an awaiting coroutine. Delayed landing cleanup uses a target-bound
callable that automatically disconnects when the ball is freed.
