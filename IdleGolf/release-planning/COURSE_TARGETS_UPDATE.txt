Implemented

All three courses now contain five destructible targets. The final target is
explicitly identified with target_id, target_name and is_final_target. Yardage
labels identify it as "Final target". Scenery beyond it is not a completion goal.

Course 1 / Big Piney retains its existing five targets and tuning. Its Mountain
at z=1500 is the final target (500 hits), ID level1_destructible3.

These distances are world-space forward Z, not displayed yards. The existing
yardage conversion is 1.094. Final-target displays are approximately 1,641,
2,188 and 2,625 yards respectively.

Desert targets replace their original decorative instances. Cacti and the pump
are offset away from the sidebar/shop. Each pyramid has a separate physical
hitbox on its exposed face; all three share one damage count, payout and respawn.
Destructible.gd now handles every CollisionShape3D under its StaticBody3D, while
still counting each ball once per target life. Course isolation applies to all
of these shapes. Existing damage/reward rules and save schema are unchanged.

The beach uses its existing separate sky, water, island and fairway layers.
BeachTarget.gdshader masks the ship and lighthouse out of the original composite
illustration for independent rendering. The PNG is untouched. They disappear
when broken, revealing clean scenery rather than another baked-in copy. Other
beach targets reuse existing target, water-tower and balloon artwork.

Reachability checks launch Standard Balls with the Standard Golfer through the
real main._hit_ball path. They use legal aim/loft controls, normal gravity/damping,
no teleporting, and no direct damage callbacks. Every one of the 17 hitboxes
receives two actual shots. Other targets stay active after verified respawn.
Partial-damage fixtures leave two hits per hitbox before the authored threshold;
the final real hit must destroy the target and pay a reward. The tests also check
all colliders disable, respawn clears damage, and all colliders restore. Existing
course tests now query all hitboxes through repeated course switches.

Test stats deliberately isolate geometry: consistency level 95, consistency medal
level 10, zero XP stat bonuses, and finite cash speed upgrades. Final target speed
levels in this fixture: Mountain 534, Pyramids 606-626 across its three faces,
Lighthouse 749. These are physics witnesses, NOT recommended balance or proof
that current upgrade prices are achievable in the desired playtime. C05 must
model carried-over progression, costs, distances and health together.

The longer reachability harness is paced to at most 1,000 frames/second with
60 Hz simulated physics. An uncapped development run hit a Jolt job-queue warning;
it was rejected by the diagnostics gate. The paced final run passed without that
warning; the gate was not weakened.
