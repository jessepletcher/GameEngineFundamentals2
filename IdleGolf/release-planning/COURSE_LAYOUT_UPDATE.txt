COURSE LAYOUT CORRECTIONS
October 23, 2026

Owner clarification: remove only the added objects, retain original scenery as
destructibles, and make the cargo ship the Beach completion target.

Changes

Removed the added Beach practice target, water tower and balloon, including their
physics shapes and inherited-scene overrides. Big Piney's original five targets
remain. Desert's five original scenery objects remain destructible. Beach now
has its original ship and lighthouse only: 12 targets and 14 hitboxes overall.

The lighthouse moved from forward z=2400 to z=90 (about 98 displayed yards).
Its visual size and original place in the illustration remain; its physical
body and yardage anchor are scaled to the new nearshore distance. It is now an
ordinary target, with 25 hits and 3 base money per hit.

The Cargo Ship is the Beach's final target, ID level3_cargoship, with 500 hits
and 15 base money per hit. Its distant-horizon plane is z=1700 (about 1,860
displayed yards); its hitbox is scaled with the authored artwork. This final
distance was checked against real-shot reachability and the retained pacing
target. It replaces the lighthouse's previous final-target role.

Desert cacti and the pump return to their original image registration (no added
horizontal pixel offsets). Both foreground cacti are at z=45 and the pump at
z=100. Their physical sizes and positions now match that depth and visible solid
parts of their artwork. The sand backdrop is behind the object layers, before
the sky. Foreground grass, cacti, pump/palms, dunes/hills, pyramids and clouds
retain the original composition and shared authored canvas scale. PNGs untouched.

Completion and saves

Beach's completion UI and target registry now name the Cargo Ship. Breaking the
lighthouse cannot award a new Beach completion. Course milestones are stored per
course, so previously earned Beach completion is preserved; no save reset or
schema change is required. Completion still survives retirement and restarting.
Sandbox and balance launcher instructions now refer to the ship.
