IDLE GOLF - C02 GOLFER ROSTER REVIEW
October 23, 2026

Implementation

All eight retained golfers now have distinct five-pose swing sheets and actual
golfer portraits. Standard Golfer, Shop Keep and Lion Trees keep their existing
art. Dyson and Blue Collar no longer borrow the default golfer's sheet. Zoomer,
Lamb and Money Daily now animate instead of releasing immediately from a still.
All five former golf-ball portrait placeholders use matching character crops.

One elapsed-time timeline controls the poses and ball release. Contact occurs
at the fourth pose (60% of the swing), once per swing, including when a slow
frame crosses the contact point. The final pose returns to the ready stance.
Animation speed includes the golfer's rate passive and remains shorter than
the automatic shot interval at high upgrade levels. Duplicate swing requests
and closing the shop cannot restart a windup or create an extra shot.

Equipping a different golfer or changing course cancels a pending windup. The
next scheduled swing uses current equipment; already airborne balls retain
their original launch stats. Disabling spawning also cancels pending contact.
Removed stale scene signal connections and frame-change-based release logic.
Registered atlas positions keep the new golfers' feet aligned across poses,
and normalized world scale keeps their size consistent with the original art.

shot's captured golfer determines this delay, even after equipment changes.

Passive values are retained; Dyson/Lamb now also affect animation speed.

Money Daily previously cost 35 medals at level 14, after Shop Keep's stronger
money bonus at 20 medals/level 10. Money Daily is now an earlier money option:
12 medals at level 6. Existing ownership/equipment IDs and saved progress remain
valid. Other golfer prices, unlocks and demo restrictions are unchanged.
Short descriptions replace inaccurate distance claims and unfinished wording.
