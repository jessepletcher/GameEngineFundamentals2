COSMETIC FRAGMENT ISOLATION - B09
October 23, 2026

Historical B09 report. B10 Market persistence is now implemented; see
MARKET_PERSISTENCE_UPDATE.txt for the current save and transition behavior.

Completed in source with Godot 4.6 stable. Existing Windows exports need rebuilding
to include B06-B09. No export, Steam upload or native visual QA was performed.

Behavior

- The two decorative fragments from each Firework explosion no longer collide
  with ground, destructibles or other balls. Collision layer/mask are both zero,
  and their CollisionShape3D is disabled before their first physics step.
- Cosmetic fragments are excluded from the balls gameplay group. Destructibles
  also explicitly require can_damage_targets(), so accidental group membership
  cannot enable cosmetic damage.
- Gameplay payout/chain callbacks reject cosmetic fragments. They cannot acquire
  homing targets, explode into scoring children, consume golden flags or invest.
- Both scoring and cosmetic objects belong to a shots lifecycle group. Switching
  courses cancels/removes all of them immediately, including cosmetic objects
  that are no longer found by gameplay queries.
- Decorative fragments retain gravity-driven trail motion. At the visual landing
  threshold they freeze, stop emissions/processing, and free after 0.5 seconds.
  No scoring landing signal is emitted. Stopping processing also prevents trail
  emissions from restarting during the fade.
- Real fragments still collide and damage destructibles. Fragment counts, B08
  payout rules and near/distant scoring compensation are unchanged.

Implementation

idle-golf/Ball.gd: gameplay capability guard, cosmetic physics isolation, separate
group membership, homing/explosion rejection and cosmetic landing cleanup.
idle-golf/Destructible.gd: capability check before counting a proximity hit.
idle-golf/main.gd: use shots for course cleanup, reject cosmetics at reward entry.
Tests stay outside the shipping project. Save format and paths are unchanged.
