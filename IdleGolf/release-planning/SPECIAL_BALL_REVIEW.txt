Behavior review

- Market deposits direct-hit rewards into persistent pots. Same-flag streaks
  scale deposits; money and accrued XP grow together while playing. Non-deposit
  landings, course changes and retirement cash out investments. Reload restores
  pots without offline growth. The old 10%/s description disagreed with the
  ledger's current growth rate and omitted other cashout paths.
- Pinball continues a direct-hit shot through the current course's remaining
  valid flags, retaining its launch context and stopping when the shot/course
  becomes invalid. Chain rewards do not repeat the original distance payout.
  The existing distance cleanup also limits chaining, so the old unconditional
  promise to visit every flag was too broad. Its old consistency number did
  not match the catalog value.
- Wrecking rewards destructible hits using the ball's destruction multiplier
  and each hit's original money/XP context. It does not have the distance boost
  advertised by the old description. The new line describes its intended role
  without promising extra range or faster destruction.
- Firework bursts near the apex into scoring fragments and cosmetic particles.
  Distant bursts use fewer scoring fragments with payout compensation; cosmetic
  particles cannot collide, damage or reward. The old claim of six balls at the
  apex was inaccurate. Updated a stale internal timing comment as well.
