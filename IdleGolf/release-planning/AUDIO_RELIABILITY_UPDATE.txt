AUDIO MUTE AND AMBIENCE RELIABILITY - B11
October 23, 2026

Completed in source using Godot 4.6 stable and real audio-mixer PCM tests. Existing
Windows exports need rebuilding to include B06-B11. No export or Steam upload was
performed. Output was verified inside Godot's mixer; physical speaker/device and
subjective listening QA were not performed in this batch.

Fixed behavior

- Saved SFX/music mute is applied when AudioManager initializes. Background stream
  requests are retained even when muted, and unmute starts streams that had never
  played. Already-playing streams pause and resume at their existing positions.
- AudioManager now owns the combined pause decision: user music mute OR shop open.
  Unmuting in the shop cannot bypass its pause, and leaving the shop cannot bypass
  the user's mute setting. Music mute continues to include ambience.
- Main no longer directly manipulates background players or runs a wall-clock
  ambience restart timer. AudioManager monitors the actual playback position and
  seeks back at the trimmed end, preserving the existing final-20-second skip.
  Muting/shop visits do not advance this boundary or restart paused ambience.
- Non-looping background streams restart after their finished signal. Short
  ambience streams use their full length instead of creating a negative interval.
- Re-requesting the same track during a scene reload retains its playback state.
  New scene initialization clears stale shop pause/ducking and applies saved mute.
- SFX mute stops current pooled sounds and stops/frees temporary overflow players.
  Unmuting permits new effects without replaying old ones.
- Shop ducking applies -20 dB only to current/future SFX players. It no longer
  writes the Master bus volume or changes background volume settings.
- Background stream cleanup remains idempotent and releases streams on shutdown.

Implementation

idle-golf/AudioManager.gd owns requested streams, mute/shop state, playback sync,
ambience trim looping, SFX stopping and per-player ducking.
idle-golf/main.gd uses set_shop_open() and play_ambience(stream, 20.0), replacing
direct stream_paused changes and the scene timer. Save schema remains version 2;
existing settings fields and B10 investment persistence are unchanged.
