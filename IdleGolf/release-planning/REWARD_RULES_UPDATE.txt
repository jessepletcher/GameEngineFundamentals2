Corrections

- Every gameplay reward now includes the catalog ball money multiplier: Homing
  1.5x, Firework 0.5x, Pinball 0.7x, all others 1x. These values previously existed
  in the catalog but never reached the payout path.
- Money upgrades, level bonuses, golfer money and ball money multiply together
  exactly once, captured at launch. XP is final earned money x 0.1 x launch XP
  multiplier. B07 equipment/course-switching guarantees remain in place.
- Ball flag multipliers apply to ordinary, chain and golden flag bonuses. Pin
  Seeker's 2x flag bonus and Market's 1.5x now apply to golden rewards as well.
- Golden fragment rewards now retain the same fragment-count compensation as
  ordinary landing rewards. Destructible contributions remain per physical hit;
  compensation does not increase damage or change hits required to break a target.
- Money, lifetime money and final XP are credited through one GameState method.
  Market cashout credits its already-accrued money/XP together; its callers no
  longer credit money separately. Current equipment cannot multiply a pot again.
- Help includes the corrected money formula, golden reward rule and a ball reward
  table generated directly from catalog values.

Reward contract

M = (1 + money-upgrade-level x 0.09) x money-level-bonus x golfer-money x ball-money
X = (1 + XP-upgrade-level x 0.09) x XP-level-bonus
F = max(flag forward distance, 0) x 0.1 x flag distance multiplier x accuracy
Accuracy is clamped to 0..1. Ordinary landing takes the best matching flag bonus.

Landing: (yards^2 x 0.0002 + 5 + F x ball-flag-mult) x direct-factor x M x C
  Direct-factor = 2.5 for a direct hit, otherwise 1.
Pinball arrival: F x ball-flag-mult x 2.5 x M
  Chain arrivals do not earn another base/distance payment.
Golden extra: F x ball-flag-mult x 50 x M x C
  Separate from the ordinary landing/chain payment, with no extra 2.5 multiplier.
Destructible hit: money-per-hit x target-distance-in-yards x ball-destruct-mult x M
  Destruction pays accumulated money/XP from the individual hits.
XP for each reward: final money x 0.1 x X
C = 1 normally, or 3/scoring-fragment-count for Firework landing/golden rewards.
  Near fireworks: 3 scoring fragments at 1x. Distant: 2 at 1.5x each.
Market: direct landing reward is deposited, scaled by the same-flag streak count.
  Money and XP grow together under the existing 20%/second update. No new bonus
  applies when cashing out. Golden extras still pay immediately.

Example: a 100-yard miss with a standard golfer, no upgrades or level bonuses:
  Standard $7 / 0.7 XP; Homing $10.50 / 1.05 XP;
  Firework fragment $3.50 / 0.35 XP before fragment compensation;
  Pinball $4.90 / 0.49 XP.

Implementation

idle-golf/RewardRules.gd contains pure calculations for combined money multipliers,
base rewards, flag accuracy/distance, landings, pinball, golden flags, per-hit
destructible contributions and scaling accrued rewards. It has no live GameState
dependency and returns finalized money/XP pairs.
GameState.credit_reward applies a pair without further multipliers. add_money
delegates through the same rules. Refunds and purchases remain balance transfers
and do not award XP or lifetime earnings.
Main, Flag and Destructible route their reward calculations through RewardRules.
Flag's golden signal carries the unmultiplied flag bonus plus fragment compensation.
Save schema and paths are unchanged; no historical earnings are recalculated.
