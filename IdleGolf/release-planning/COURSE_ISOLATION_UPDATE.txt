Behavior

- Course switching sets both scenery/destructible and flag branches to disabled
  processing while hidden, restoring inherited processing when selected.
- Descendant scripts, animations and node-bound tweens pause. Destructible bodies
  explicitly use DISABLE_MODE_REMOVE, so disabled course bodies leave physics.
  Tests use actual physics-space overlap queries, not just visibility/property checks.
- The shared ground and golfer remain active across switches.
- Destructibles reject hidden/dead hit and crumble callbacks; reward signals are
  also checked at the receiving end because disabled nodes can still emit signals.
- Flags reject inactive hit/golden/wiggle requests. Golden reward callbacks require
  the current course's flag branch. Existing switch-time golden cleanup remains.
- Homing chooses only processing flags. If an airborne ball's selected target is
  disabled, homing stops and clears the target. It does not automatically retarget.
- Hidden destructibles are excluded from yardage announcements and warning rings.
- Partial damage survives switching. Destruction/respawn animations freeze while
  away and resume on return; switching does not instantly restore destroyed targets.
- Pinball validates course identity before and after each awaited flight. A switch
  invalidates pending payouts even after an immediate return to the original course.
  The current tween finishes, then the stale chain despawns without awarding it.

Implementation

idle-golf/main.gd: branch activation, source-checked rewards, chain revision guard.
idle-golf/Ball.gd: active homing candidates, cached-target invalidation, warning filter.
idle-golf/Destructible.gd: physics removal policy, guarded damage and announcements.
idle-golf/Flag.gd: guarded hit/golden/wiggle entry points.
All current course descendants inherit processing; future course additions must
remain under their course branches and retain that processing/physics contract.
Flags/destructibles stay in their registry groups so initialization can connect
signals for every course. Gameplay lookups explicitly filter inactive members.
